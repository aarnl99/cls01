Common Questions

Things planners ask us.

How early should we book transportation for Snowflake Summit? +
As early as you can. Hotels, venues, and vehicles all get booked up during big conference weeks in the Bay Area. For groups of 100 or more, we suggest reaching out at least two to three months ahead. If you are closer than that, call us anyway and we will tell you what is open.
Can you run a shuttle loop between our hotel and the venue? +
Yes. We plan the loop around your session times, pick the right size vehicles, and put it in a written run plan before the event. Every vehicle is tracked live with GPS, and our dispatch team watches the whole thing.
Do you handle SFO, OAK, and SJC airport pickups? +
Yes. We can meet arriving guests, speakers, and executives at any Bay Area airport and bring them to their hotel or straight to the venue.
Is CLS the official transportation provider for this event? +
No. CLS Global Transportation is an independent Bay Area transportation company. We are not affiliated with Snowflake or Snowflake Summit 2027. We work directly with companies, sponsors, and groups who are attending and need their own rides.
Can you provide a certificate of insurance for our venue? +
Yes. CLS carries full commercial insurance and can name your venue or company as an additional insured, often the same day you ask.
