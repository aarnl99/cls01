Our Process

How we plan an event from scratch.

Event transportation has a lot of moving pieces. Here's how CLS keeps them from becoming your problem.

01

One discovery call

We talk through your event, date, headcount, pickup and drop-off locations, timing constraints, and any special requests. Most clients have their transportation plan in place after this one call. We've done enough events that we know what questions to ask before you do.

02

We write a run plan

CLS maps every vehicle, every route, every pickup window, and every driver assignment for your event. You get a written run plan to review before anything is confirmed. Staging areas, load times, contingency vehicles, all of it documented so there are no surprises on event day.

03

Day-of dispatch runs everything

On event day, CLS dispatch monitors all vehicles in real time via Samsara GPS. If a bus runs late or a route needs to adjust, we handle it before you even know it happened. Your event coordinator has one phone number to call if they need us, and we pick up.

Event Day

What event day actually looks like with CLS.

For a corporate conference with 800 attendees arriving over two days at a hotel shuttle loop, here's what we run.

48 HOURS OUT

Driver briefing and vehicle inspection

All assigned drivers receive the event run plan, venue contact info, and any specific instructions. Every vehicle gets a pre-trip inspection. Backup vehicles confirmed on standby.

DAY OF, 2 HOURS BEFORE FIRST RUN

Drivers stage at designated locations

CLS drivers arrive at their assigned staging points early. Dispatch opens the live GPS feed and confirms all vehicles are in position. Your on-site coordinator gets a check-in call from CLS.

DURING THE EVENT

Continuous dispatch monitoring

All vehicles tracked live. CLS dispatch adjusts run times based on actual passenger load and any venue timing changes. If a bus fills early or a session runs long, we add a run.

END OF EVENT

Last-run coordination and clearance

Dispatch confirms all guests are loaded and all vehicles are en route to the final drop. No one gets left at the venue. CLS provides a run report within 24 hours if you need it for your recap.

Common Questions

Things people ask before they book.

Still have questions? Call us at 650.460.8778.

How far in advance do I need to book? +
For large events (100 or more guests), we recommend reaching out four to six weeks ahead. For private charters and smaller groups, two weeks is usually enough. That said, we handle last-minute bookings regularly, if you're in a bind, call us first and we'll tell you what's available.
What's your minimum booking for a private charter? +
Our minimum charter booking is three hours, which covers most half-day wine tours and local events. For multi-day or overnight trips, we quote based on total hours including deadhead and overnight positioning as needed.
Can you provide a certificate of insurance for our venue? +
Yes. CLS carries full commercial insurance and we can name your venue or organization as an additional insured on the certificate. Most venues have their requirements, we've seen them all and can usually turn a COI around the same day you need it.
What happens if a driver or vehicle has a problem on event day? +
Our dispatch team monitors all events from a central operations center and keeps backup capacity available. If a vehicle has a mechanical issue or a driver is unavailable, we have a replacement ready. In 30 years of Bay Area events, we have never canceled a confirmed job.
Do you charge extra for waiting time or late events? +
For charter bookings, waiting time beyond the contracted hours is billed at an hourly rate that we quote upfront. For events with a scheduled end time, we build buffer into the plan. We don't charge surprise fees, every cost is discussed before you sign.
Can you handle alcohol on charter trips (wine tours, etc.)? +
Passengers 21 and over may have sealed or open containers on charter vehicles, depending on the jurisdiction and vehicle type. For wine tours, we allow guests to bring open bottles onto the coach between stops. We ask that passengers remain respectful of the vehicle and driver, groups that become disruptive may be asked to end the trip.
Do you offer ADA accessible vehicles for events? +
Yes. CLS has ADA accessible coaches with ramps or wheelchair lifts. Let us know during the planning process and we'll include the right vehicles in your run plan. If you're not sure what you need, tell us the number of guests with mobility considerations and we'll figure it out together.
