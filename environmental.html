Our Commitment
An environmental leader in ground transportation.

CLS Global joined Green Ride Global in May 2013 becoming one of the first ground transportation companies in Northern California to make a formal, third-party verified commitment to GHG emissions reduction.

We carefully track and monitor our emissions across all areas of operations. Every route we operate removes private vehicles from Bay Area roads, making a measurable impact on regional air quality.

Our Carbon Accounting procedures follow IPCC emissions factors and ISO 14064 GHG Measurement Standards, the same standards used by the world leading sustainability organizations.

Gold Member of Green Ride Global
Through our independent third-party relationship with GRG, we are held environmentally accountable and issue detailed environmental reports documenting the carbon footprint from fleet operations.
Environmental Program
What our program includes.
Comprehensive Environmental Policy
A formal policy identifying and addressing the reduction of potential environmental impacts from all areas of business operations, not just fleet emissions.
Fleet GHG Inventory and Monitoring
Ongoing measurement and monitoring of fleet greenhouse gas emissions tracked by route, vehicle, and time period. Available for client review at any time.
Low-Carbon Fleet Transition
Continuous transition to the latest low-carbon vehicles and fuels including EV and hybrid options available for any program.
Greening of the Supply Chain
Environmental accountability extends beyond our vehicles to our vendors, contractors, and operational partners. We hold our supply chain to the same standards.
Reporting
Carbon footprint reporting for your organization.

We provide clients with trip-specific GHG emissions reporting at no additional cost. Meets the requirements of Fortune 500 companies and government agencies.

Trip-Specific Emissions Reports
GHG emissions data for any specific trip, route, or time period formatted for corporate sustainability reports, board presentations, or government filings.
Monthly and Annual GHG Reports
Comprehensive monthly and annual reports covering total fleet emissions, per-route breakdown, cars-off-road equivalent, and year-over-year reduction tracking.
Third-Party Verified
All reports are verified through Green Ride Global ensuring credibility for Fortune 500 supply chain audits, ESG disclosures, and government contracts.
IPCC and ISO 14064 Standards
Our carbon accounting follows emissions factors from the IPCC and GHG Measurement Standards from ISO 14064, the gold standard for enterprise and government sustainability reporting.
WBCSD Protocol Compliant
Our measurements follow the principles and protocols of the World Business Council for Sustainable Development compatible with any major corporate ESG framework.
No Additional Cost
GHG reporting is included in all CLS programs at no additional charge, a meaningful benefit for any sustainability team managing supply chain emissions data.
