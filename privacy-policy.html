Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

CLS Global Transportation, Inc. ("CLS", "we", "us" or "our") operates the clsgt.com website and the CLS rider app (together, the "Service"). This page explains what personal information we collect when you use the Service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you call, text or email us, or use a quote or request form on this site, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, company, trip details and any message you write. When you send a form on this site, our website host (Netlify) stores what you entered and passes it to our team so we can reply.

Usage data. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you view and the time of your visit. We also use Google Analytics to count visits, see how people find our site, and see which pages lead to a quote request or a call. Google Analytics uses cookies and gives us totals, not your name. You can learn how Google uses this data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out with Google's browser add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. When you use our rider app, it may collect device information and, if you allow it, location data needed to show shuttle arrival times.

Cookies. This website does not use advertising cookies. Google Analytics and some third party services (such as web fonts) use cookies or similar technology to work properly. You can set your browser to refuse cookies.

How we use information

  • To answer your questions and prepare quotes
  • To provide, run and support our transportation services
  • To let you know about changes to our services
  • To keep our Service safe and fix technical problems
  • To understand how our Service is used so we can improve it

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing information

We share information only with companies that help us run the Service (for example our website host, email provider, and dispatch and rider app platforms), and only so they can do that work for us. We may also disclose information when we believe in good faith it is needed to comply with the law, protect the rights or property of CLS, investigate possible wrongdoing, or protect the safety of our riders or the public.

Keeping information safe

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of sending or storing data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where information is stored

Your information is processed in the United States. If you contact us from outside the United States, you agree to your information being transferred to and processed in the United States.

Links to other sites

Our Service may link to sites we do not run. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies.

Children

Our Service is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, by contacting us below. California residents may have additional rights under California law.

Changes to this policy
