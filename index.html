The challenge

Adobe needed to consolidate commuter routes across its North San Jose campus, reduce its reliance on three separate carriers, and get real-time ridership data to inform future real estate decisions. The existing setup had no single point of contact and no consistent reporting.

What CLS built

CLS consolidated all six routes under a single managed program, deployed a mix of 40-passenger coaches and 24-passenger sprinter vans depending on demand by time of day, and integrated our Tripshot dispatch platform so Adobe's facilities team got daily ridership dashboards and automated exception alerts. One account manager. One invoice. Weekly reviews.

The challenge

Levi's Plaza manages a large mixed-use campus in San Francisco's Embarcadero neighborhood with tenants spread across multiple buildings and shift patterns. Their previous transportation setup used four different vendors, each with their own billing, scheduling system, and driver pool, making it nearly impossible to manage changes quickly or hold anyone accountable.

What CLS built

CLS took over all four vendor contracts and rebuilt the program under a single managed operation, standardizing vehicle types, driver uniforms, and rider communication across all buildings. We added an on-demand overflow service for late shifts and a direct line to our SF dispatch office so facilities managers could reach a person, not a ticket system, any time of day.

The challenge

Neuralink's Fremont facility draws engineers and researchers from across the Bay Area, many of whom rely on BART and Caltrain. Getting employees from transit hubs to the facility safely and on time, without asking them to manage their own rideshare logistics, was a priority as the team scaled rapidly and needed to focus every hour on the work.

What CLS built

CLS designed a last-mile network connecting BART's Fremont station and the Millbrae Caltrain hub directly to the facility, with timed departures synced to train arrivals and a morning and evening peak schedule. All drivers are TSI-certified, full background checked, and operate under our safety monitoring platform. The program was designed, staffed, and running in under three weeks from contract signing.
