How It Works

From first call to running program
in three steps.

We've launched commuter programs for companies of every size. Here's how a typical program goes from idea to live service.

01

Tell us your situation

Share your office locations, headcount, neighborhood data, and shift times on one call. We've done this for dozens of Bay Area companies, we know the right questions to ask. No RFP required to get started.

02

Service Types

What kind of program do you need?

Every Bay Area company has a different situation. We build around yours.

Home to Work Shuttles

Daily door-near service from residential pickup zones to your office. Routes are built around where your employees actually live, using real address data to maximize ridership and minimize empty seats.

BART & Caltrain Connections

Last-mile shuttles from transit hubs straight to your campus. Timed to train arrivals so employees step off the train and onto your shuttle without waiting. We watch the real-time feed and adjust when trains run late.

Inter-Campus Routes

Fixed-schedule shuttles between your office locations, parking structures, and nearby amenities. Keeps employees moving between buildings without needing a car mid-day.

Shared Commuter Programs

If your office park or business district has multiple tenants, a shared shuttle program can cut each company's cost by 30 to 50 percent. CLS manages the route, the billing split, and the communication to all groups.

Park & Ride Service

Solve parking overflow by shuttling employees from an off-site lot to your front door. Reduces on-site congestion, reduces your parking spend, and gives employees a stress-free start to the day.

Executive & VIP Transport

Premium vehicle service for executive teams, board visitors, and recurring VIP runs. Sedans, SUVs, and Sprinter vans with professional drivers who know how to represent your company.

Common Questions

Questions we get a lot.

If you don't see yours here, just call us at 650.460.8778.

How long does it take to launch a new shuttle program? +
Most programs are up and running within two to four weeks of signing. We've launched programs in under a week for clients with urgent timelines. The main factors are finalizing routes, vehicle availability, and getting your rider communications out.
What's the minimum size for a CLS shuttle program? +
There's no hard minimum. We've run programs for companies with 50 daily riders and programs with thousands. If you have a genuine commuter challenge, we'll build something that makes financial sense at your scale.
What if our headcount or office changes? +
We adjust. Route changes, vehicle upgrades or downgrades, added stops, and schedule changes are all handled by your account manager. You don't have to go back out to bid every time something changes.
Do you handle rider communications and app setup? +
Yes. CLS sets up the rider app, sends launch communications to your employees, and manages ongoing support questions from riders. Your HR or facilities team doesn't need to babysit it.
How do you handle driver shortages or vehicle issues on the day of service? +
We maintain backup driver and vehicle capacity across our Bay Area operations. If a vehicle has a mechanical issue or a driver calls in sick, our dispatch team covers it without your team needing to know. That's what fully managed means.
Can you help us with a pre-tax commuter benefit program? +
Yes. We work with your HR team to structure the program so employees can use pre-tax commuter dollars (up to the IRS limit) to cover their shuttle pass. This reduces net cost for employees and removes payroll tax for the company on that benefit.
Are CLS drivers employees or contractors? +
CLS drivers are CLS employees, not gig workers or subcontractors. They go through our training program, drug testing, and background checks. They wear our uniform and represent CLS every day. That's a meaningful difference in accountability.
