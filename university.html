Program Types

What we run for colleges and institutions.

From daily intercampus loops to special event service, CLS has built programs for every kind of campus transportation need.

🏫

Intercampus Shuttles

Fixed-route service connecting your main campus, satellite locations, research buildings, and parking structures. Scheduled to match class periods and shift changes so riders always have a reliable run.

🚉

Transit Connection Shuttles

Last-mile service from BART, Caltrain, and ACE stations to campus. Timed to actual train arrivals, not just a schedule. Reduces parking demand and makes campus accessible to more students and staff.

🌙

Late Night & Safety Routes

On-demand or fixed late-night routes for students traveling after dark. Covers library close, lab hours, and campus events. Helps meet your campus safety and student welfare commitments.

🅿️

Remote Parking Shuttles

Loop service between overflow and remote parking lots and the core campus. Reduces congestion at peak arrival times and makes remote lots a real option for commuters, not a punishment.

♿

ADA & Paratransit Service

Accessible vehicles with ramps and lifts, drivers trained in ADA service protocols, and scheduling that integrates with your disability services office. CLS meets ADA requirements for public and private institutions.

🎓

Special Event & Sports Transport

Charter service for commencement, athletics, field trips, and institutional events. CLS provides buses, drivers, and event day coordination, all under the same account manager who runs your daily program.

CLS operates accessible vehicles and drivers trained in ADA service standards. We help your institution meet its legal obligations and serve every student and staff member.

Ridership reporting you can use

How It Works

Getting a campus program started.

Campus transportation often involves a procurement process. Here's how CLS works through it with you.

01

Discovery, understand your campus

We walk your routes, review your current service (if any), understand your ADA requirements, and learn your student and staff populations. Most campus programs are more complex than they look on paper, we do the homework first.

02

Proposal, routes, vehicles, and pricing

CLS puts together a written proposal with route maps, vehicle specifications, driver schedules, and fully loaded pricing. If your institution requires a formal RFP process, we work through it. If not, we can move faster.

03

Launch, with training and communications

CLS sets up rider communication (app, signage, route maps), trains your on-campus contacts, and runs a soft launch to work out any timing issues before the full semester starts. We iron out the details before students notice them.

04

Ongoing, your program, managed daily

After launch, CLS handles daily dispatch, driver scheduling, and rider support. You get a monthly report and a dedicated contact. At the start of each semester, we review ridership and adjust service levels with you.

Common Questions

Questions from campus transportation teams.

Call us at 650.460.8778 if yours isn't here.

Can CLS work with our institution's procurement process? +
Yes. CLS has gone through formal RFP and bid processes for public and private institutions. We provide all required documentation including insurance certificates, references, safety records, and pricing breakdowns in whatever format your process requires.
How do you handle service during finals, breaks, and summer? +
We build academic calendar service levels into the program from the start. Reduced service during breaks and summer, increased during high-demand periods, and special event coverage for commencement and major events, all planned in advance so nothing falls through.
Do CLS drivers receive training for working in campus environments? +
Yes. Campus drivers get specific training on your campus layout, pedestrian zones, loading areas, and any rules your institution has for vehicles on campus. They also go through CLS's standard DOT training, background check, and drug screening program.
What reporting do you provide? +
Monthly reports cover per-route ridership, on-time performance, total passenger trips, and fleet utilization. We can format reports to match your internal templates or sustainability reporting requirements (AASHE STARS, etc.). Annual emissions reports are included for programs that need them.
Can you integrate with our existing transit or wayfinding app? +
CLS uses Tripshot and Ride Systems as its dispatch platform, and our Samsara GPS data can be shared via GTFS-RT feeds for integration with campus wayfinding systems. Talk to us about your specific platform and we'll find out what's possible.
