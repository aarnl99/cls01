We started on the ground. We've stayed there.

CLS was founded in the Bay Area in 1991 with one goal: run transportation programs the right way. Not brokered out. Not handed off to a dispatch number. Actually run, with drivers who knew the routes, dispatchers who watched every trip, and a team that treated each program as their own.

Over 30 years, that approach hasn't changed. What's changed is the technology and the scale. We now serve millions of passengers a year, across corporate campuses, universities, government agencies, and private events up and down California.

We're not the biggest company in the space. That's on purpose. We take on clients where we can actually deliver. That means dedicated staff on your account, not a shared inbox, and a team that knows your routes, your riders, and your standards.

What we actually believe about transportation.

Most operators treat a shuttle program as a contract. You get vehicles, a dispatch number, and a report once a month. If something goes wrong, you find out from a rider complaint.

That's not how we see it. When a company runs a shuttle program, they're making a promise to the people who ride it every day. We're the ones who keep that promise. Every missed pickup, every delay, every late departure reflects on the company that hired us. We take that personally.

Your program is yours. Our job is to run it better than you'd run it yourself, and to prove it every week with data.
